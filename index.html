Services

Check-up & Consultation

Routine oral examination, diagnosis, and treatment planning for new and returning patients

Scaling & Polishing

Removal of plaque and tartar buildup along the gumline, followed by polishing to smooth tooth surfaces

Invisalign

Clear, removable aligner treatment for straightening teeth as an alternative to traditional braces; fitted and monitored by a registered Invisalign Provider

Teeth Whitening

In-clinic professional whitening treatment to reduce surface staining and discolouration

Tooth Filling

Composite (tooth-coloured) resin fillings for cavities and minor structural damage

Root Canal Treatment

Treatment to remove infected or damaged pulp from inside a tooth and preserve the remaining structure

Dental Crowns & Bridges

Fixed restorations to rebuild a damaged tooth or replace one or more missing teeth

Dentures

Removable full or partial dentures to replace missing teeth and restore chewing function and appearance

Extractions

Removal of decayed, damaged, or problematic teeth, including surgical and non-surgical wisdom tooth extraction

Children's Dentistry

Check-ups, cleaning, and preventive care for younger patients, with TV displays in treatment rooms to help them feel at ease

Dental X-Ray

On-site diagnostic imaging to support accurate diagnosis and treatment planning

Emergency Dental Care

Treatment for urgent dental pain, trauma, or sudden complications
